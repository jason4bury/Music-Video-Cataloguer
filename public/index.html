      Help

      Finding videos

      Search by artist or title at the top. Click an artist, tag or playlist on the left to filter, and click it again to clear it. ★ Favourites shows only your favourites. Sort changes the order, and Least played puts the videos you've played least (or longest ago) first.

      Playing

      Click a video to play it. Next and Back step through the videos shown on the page.

      ▶ Play all plays every video shown, in the order shown, one after another. 🔀 Shuffle plays them in a fair shuffle: the least played come first, so everything gets a turn before anything repeats, and the same artist isn't played twice in a row where that can be avoided. Filter first (an artist, tag, playlist or favourites) to play just those.

      The title shows on the video for a few seconds when it starts, and Up next shows what's coming. In full screen the next title also appears near the end of each video.

      If a video can't be played (the file has gone, or the browser can't show its format), it's skipped during Play all and Shuffle. MP4 (H.264) and WebM play best.

      A video counts as played, and as watched, once it has run for 5 seconds.

      Keys while a video is open

      Space or KPause or play
      NNext video
      BBack (start again, or the previous video if near the start)
      FFull screen on or off
      MMute on or off
      ← →Back or forward 10 seconds
      EscLeave full screen, then close the player

      The media keys on your keyboard or headphones (play/pause, next, previous) work too, in browsers that support them.

      Tags, favourites and playlists

      Under the player you can mark a favourite, tick Watched, add or remove tags, and add the video to a playlist. The + on a thumbnail adds a video to a playlist without opening it. While a playlist is shown, the player can take the video out of it again.

      Look

      Look at the top switches between Dark (the usual look), Light, and System (follows your device). This browser remembers the choice.
